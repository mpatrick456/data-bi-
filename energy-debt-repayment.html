Project 04 · Energy Debt Repayment Analysis

Dashboard for debt exposure, repayment behaviour and operational prioritisation.

This Power BI case study shows how credit management data can be used to understand repayment risk, monitor debt recovery performance and help teams prioritise follow-up actions.

Background

Why this dashboard matters

Credit management teams need more than a static debt balance report. They need to know which accounts are improving, which repayment arrangements are weakening and where intervention is most likely to reduce risk.

This case study presents a Power BI dashboard as a practical work tool for reviewing debt exposure, repayment performance, overdue behaviour and customer segmentation. The purpose is to support weekly decision-making and targeted follow-up.

Business users

  • Credit management teams
  • Collections and debt recovery operations
  • Finance reporting users
  • Customer operations managers
  • Business analysts supporting operational applications

Problem statement

From debt reporting to repayment action

Debt exposure

Understand how debt is distributed by customer type, risk segment, balance band or payment behaviour.

Repayment performance

Compare expected and actual repayment progress to highlight weakening plans and missed instalments.

Operational follow-up

Create a prioritised view of accounts needing action, including high-balance accounts and broken arrangements.

Dashboard storytelling structure

Page / area Purpose Example insight Business action
Portfolio overview Summarise total debt, account volume and key risk indicators. Debt balance trend, account count, average balance and repayment status. Understand overall exposure and management focus.
Repayment behaviour Track whether repayment plans are on track or falling behind. Paid versus expected, missed payments, underpayment and broken arrangements. Prioritise intervention before debt becomes harder to recover.
Customer segmentation Group accounts by risk, balance, payment type or customer characteristics. Segments with high balances and weak repayment behaviour. Tailor contact strategy and collection treatment.
Exception follow-up Provide a practical work queue for the credit team. High balance, overdue, missed instalment and repeated failure cases. Create weekly action lists and track outcomes.

Measures and analysis ideas

  • Total outstanding debt
  • Average debt balance
  • Repayment plan completion %
  • Paid versus expected amount
  • Missed payment count
  • Overdue balance
  • Ageing bucket
  • Priority risk category

Technical approach

How the dashboard would be built

The data model can use repayment transactions, account balances, customer attributes and date dimensions. Power Query can clean and standardise account records, while DAX measures calculate repayment performance, arrears ageing, risk categories and trend movement.

The report is designed to show business impact: reducing manual checking, improving prioritisation and helping teams focus on accounts where action is most valuable.

Recommendations from the analysis

  1. Create a weekly exception list for high-balance accounts with missed instalments.
  2. Separate customers who are paying slowly from customers who have stopped paying.
  3. Review plan affordability when repeated underpayment appears.
  4. Use segment and payment method to tailor contact strategy.
  5. Track post-contact outcomes to measure whether interventions work.
