Project 03 · Learning & Development Analytics

L&D dashboard for compliance, engagement, demand and learning operations follow-up.

This Power BI portfolio project demonstrates how learning data can support L&D, HR, compliance and operational teams. It is designed around the questions managers ask: who is overdue, which training is effective, where learner engagement is weak, and what action should be taken next.

Background

Why this dashboard matters

L&D teams often manage mandatory learning, upskilling programmes, course attendance, feedback and learner engagement through separate reports or LMS exports. This creates a familiar problem: data exists, but it is difficult for managers to see risk, prioritise action and measure whether learning investment is working.

This dashboard is built as a management tool for learning operations. It helps users move from manual spreadsheet checking to a clearer view of completion, overdue learning, course demand and improvement opportunities.

Business users

  • L&D managers and learning operations teams
  • HR business partners
  • Compliance or mandatory training owners
  • Department managers responsible for team completion
  • Learning systems / LMS reporting analysts

Problem statement

From learning records to business action

Compliance risk

Identify overdue mandatory training by department, role, location or manager so follow-up can be targeted quickly.

Engagement gap

Understand where attendance, completion, cancellation or no-show behaviour suggests low engagement or poor scheduling fit.

Learning value

Use feedback, completion patterns and demand indicators to decide which courses should be improved, scaled or retired.

Dashboard storytelling structure

Page / area Purpose Example insight Business action
Executive overview Summarise the learning health of the organisation. Completion rate, overdue volume, engagement trend, risk hotspots. Agree priority teams and reporting focus.
Compliance monitoring Track mandatory learning completion and overdue risk. Departments with rising overdue counts or repeated missed deadlines. Manager reminders, escalation and targeted support.
Engagement and demand Review attendance, no-shows, cancellations and learning demand. Courses with high demand but low attendance, or high cancellation rates. Improve scheduling, communications and course availability.
Effectiveness and improvement Connect learning activity with feedback and outcome indicators. Courses with low feedback but high mandatory attendance. Improve content, redesign delivery or retire low-value training.

Measures and analysis ideas

  • Completion rate %
  • Overdue learner count
  • Average days overdue
  • No-show rate %
  • Cancellation rate %
  • Training hours per learner / FTE
  • Average feedback score
  • Compliance risk category

Technical approach

How the dashboard would be built

The model can be structured around fact tables for learning activity and feedback, supported by employee, course and date dimensions. Power Query handles data preparation and standardisation, while DAX measures calculate completion, overdue status, attendance behaviour and trend movement.

The strongest portfolio message is that the dashboard combines learning-domain knowledge with BI delivery: data preparation, KPI design, user-friendly navigation and business recommendations.

Recommendations from the analysis

  1. Create a weekly overdue learning exception list for managers.
  2. Separate compliance risk from general learning engagement so users do not mix two different problems.
  3. Review courses with repeated cancellations or no-shows before blaming learners.
  4. Use feedback trends to prioritise course improvement, not just course popularity.
  5. Automate repeat reporting where possible to reduce manual LMS export work.
