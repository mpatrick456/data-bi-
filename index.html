Working approach

Professional approach I use as a data analyst

My approach combines recognised analytics practice with my own reporting and systems experience: start with business understanding, prepare reliable data, analyse for decision value, communicate clearly, and monitor whether the output improves the process.

1. Clarify the business decision

Define the stakeholder, the decision they need to make, the KPI definition, the frequency of review and the action expected from the report.

2. Understand the data flow

Map source systems, owners, refresh timing, joins, filters and business rules before building visuals. This reduces wrong conclusions later.

3. Prepare and validate the data

Clean and standardise fields, check duplicates, handle missing values, reconcile totals and document assumptions so the report can be trusted.

4. Extract insight, not just numbers

Use segmentation, trend analysis, exceptions, ageing, variance and drill-through to explain what changed, where the risk is and why it matters.

5. Communicate and operationalise

Design the report around user workflow, provide recommended actions, agree ownership and monitor whether the dashboard improves follow-up.
